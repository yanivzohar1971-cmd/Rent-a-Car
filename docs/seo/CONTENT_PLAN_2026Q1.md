## Internal Linking Strategy

### From Hub Landing Pages:
- Link to `/cars-for-sale` (main landing)
- Link to relevant category landing pages
- Link to individual articles

### From Articles:
- Link back to hub landing page
- Link to relevant category landing pages (e.g., `/cars-for-sale/electric`)
- Link to related guides (e.g., `/guides/car-inspection`)
- Link to main inventory search (`/cars`)

### Cross-Linking:
- Buying guides link to cost articles (e.g., "למד עוד על עלויות אחזקה")
- Cost articles link to buying guides (e.g., "למד איך לבדוק רכב לפני קנייה")

## Content Guidelines

1. **Original Content Only:** No copy/paste from other sites
2. **Helpful & Accurate:** Provide genuine value, no false promises
3. **Hebrew-First:** All content in Hebrew, with proper RTL formatting
4. **Internal Links:** Minimum 3-5 internal links per article
5. **Word Count:** 800-1500 words per article
6. **FAQs:** Include 3-5 FAQs per article when relevant

## Publishing Schedule

- **Week 1-2:** Hub landing pages + 3 articles from each hub
- **Week 3-4:** 4 more articles from each hub
- **Week 5-6:** Remaining 3 articles from each hub
- **Ongoing:** Update existing articles based on search performance
